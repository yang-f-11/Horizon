'Undruggable' cancers refer to those driven by proteins like KRAS that are difficult to inhibit with conventional small-molecule drugs.

## [Apple Migrates TrueType Hinting Interpreter to Swift](https://www.swift.org/blog/migrating-truetype-hinting-to-swift/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Apple has migrated the TrueType hinting interpreter in macOS from C to Swift, leveraging Swift's memory safety features to improve security and reliability of font rendering. This migration demonstrates Apple's commitment to adopting memory-safe languages in system-level components, reducing vulnerabilities from buffer overflows and other memory bugs that could be exploited in font processing. The TrueType hinting interpreter is a bytecode interpreter that executes font hinting instructions to optimize glyph rendering at low resolutions; the Swift rewrite required careful handling of lifetimes and ownership, with the team using Swift's experimental ownership features.

hackernews · DASD · Jun 12, 19:54 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48508726)

**Background**: TrueType hinting uses mathematical instructions to adjust font outlines so they align with a pixel grid, essential for legibility at small sizes on low-resolution screens. The TrueType interpreter executes these instructions in a virtual machine, and historically was written in C, which is prone to memory errors. Migrating to Swift, a memory-safe language, eliminates whole classes of bugs like buffer overflows.

## [Tsinghua Researcher Accuses Huawei Pangu of Plagiarizing Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen Weights](https://t.me/zaihuapd/41915) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A pre-print paper by Rui-Chong Zhang from Tsinghua University proposes the Matrix-Driven Instant Review (MDIR) method and presents a case study alleging that Huawei's Pangu large language model plagiarizes weights from Alibaba's Tongyi Qianwen, with extremely low p-values as evidence. This is significant because it raises serious ethical concerns about model stealing among China's leading AI companies, and introduces a novel detection method that could impact the entire LLM ecosystem by enabling efficient plagiarism checks. MDIR leverages matrix analysis and large deviation theory to compute rigorous p-values for weight similarity, and can run on a single PC within an hour. The method is claimed to detect plagiarism even after transformations like incremental pretraining, pruning, or permutation, while avoiding false positives.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 12, 08:07

**Background**: The Matrix-Driven Instant Review (MDIR) method compares embeddings and multi-layer weights of large language models to detect unauthorized copying. Large deviation theory is a probability framework used to assess rare events, which here helps compute the statistical significance of weight similarities. The p-value quantifies how likely the observed similarity would occur by chance, with extremely low values providing strong evidence of plagiarism.

## [Reduce AI frontend slop by constraining to Qt framework](https://envs.net/~volpe/blog/posts/reduce-slop.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A blog post proposes that constraining AI code generation to a strict UI framework like Qt significantly reduces the 'sloppy' frontend output commonly produced by LLMs, leveraging the model's familiarity with Qt's well-defined design rules. This approach offers a practical method to improve AI-generated frontend quality without requiring additional training, and it highlights the role of framework constraints in guiding LLM behavior toward more consistent and usable interfaces. The author argues that Qt's long history and clear design patterns make 'Qt application' a coherent concept in the LLM's latent space, unlike the vast design space of web development. The method involves instructing the AI to produce code that adheres strictly to Qt's conventions.

hackernews · FergusArgyll · Jun 12, 14:48 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48504912)

**Background**: Large Language Models (LLMs) often generate inconsistent or visually unappealing frontend code due to the enormous variety of design styles in their training data. Qt is a mature cross-platform UI framework with strict design rules and a consistent look and feel. By constraining the LLM to a framework like Qt, the output becomes more predictable and professional.
