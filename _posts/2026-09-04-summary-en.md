## [.name Termination: ICANN Approves Verisign's End to Third-Level .name Domains](https://neil.fraser.name/news/2026/09/03/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

On 28 July 2026 ICANN approved Verisign's proposal, first submitted on 15 April 2026, to eliminate all third-level .name registrations (x.y.name). Existing registrations are to be terminated and the related second-level domains released; registrants such as the article's author have already been notified by their registrars. This decision strips potentially long-held websites, email addresses, and device identifiers from people who registered third-level .name domains, while releasing the corresponding two-level domains for anyone to claim. Critics argue it threatens ICANN's stability mandate and opens the door to domain squatting and account-recovery failures. Only third-level .name domains are affected; registrations such as dvt.name at the second level remain valid. Verisign reported roughly 22,000 existing third-level .name registrations to ICANN, many of which are unused, and the proposal does not commit to reserving released second-level domains for a transition period.

hackernews · pavel_lishin · Sep 3, 14:54 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550772)

**Background**: .name is a generic top-level domain operated by Verisign under ICANN oversight. When .name launched in 2002, it only allowed third-level registrations such as bob.smith.name to discourage cybersquatting on surnames, and second-level .name domains became available in 2004. This history makes the termination a reversal of a long-standing policy for the namespace's remaining third-level holders.

## [Bringing a 1993 Amiga 68000 Assembly Game to Godot with an LLM](https://babyloniantwins.com/blog/porting-a-1993-amiga-game-to-godot/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A developer who originally built an Amiga game in 1993 using MC68000 assembly has ported it to the Godot game engine in a single evening, using the Claude LLM to read and convert the original assembly source into working code. He spent additional weekends polishing the game feel, and has now released the original Amiga game for free. This demonstrates that modern LLMs can act as a practical bridge between decades-old assembly code and modern game engines, potentially making retro game preservation and re-release projects far more accessible. It also offers developers a concrete workflow for reviving legacy 68k or other assembly-based codebases. The developer had the LLM repeatedly assemble the code with vasm until the resulting binary was byte-identical to the original game files, but a persistent 108-byte mismatch remained. He explains this discrepancy by noting that the original shipped files were memory snapshots saved by AsmOne after the game had already been running, not clean assembler output.

hackernews · rabahs · Sep 3, 14:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49550375)

**Background**: In the early 1990s, many Amiga games were written directly in Motorola 68000 assembly to maximize performance and conserve memory, as the MC68000 was the CPU at the heart of the Amiga. AsmOne was a popular integrated Amiga assembler and development environment for such work, while vasm is a modern portable assembler that can recreate binaries for retro targets. Using an LLM to translate or decompile legacy assembly is an emerging technique for reviving old software when original high-level source code no longer exists.

## [K2 Horizon: A Connected Fleet of Six Fully Open Models](https://ifm.ai/blog/k2/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

IFM has introduced K2 Horizon, a connected family of six fully open models that includes public source code, training data, and data-processing details. The release signals a push toward greater transparency in AI model development. Fully open models reduce the risk of hidden bias or societal manipulation that comes with closed black-box systems, giving developers and researchers a verifiable alternative. This release adds momentum to the open-model ecosystem, though performance claims still need independent validation. Community tests indicate the dense 32B model significantly trails Qwen3 27B in key benchmarks, and one reviewer found the 3.7B model unreliable for coding, as it generated incorrect code and hallucinated nonexistent APIs. Commenters also noted that the documentation's charts use tiny fonts, calling into question readability rather than technical capability.

hackernews · karimf · Sep 3, 15:36 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49551760)

**Background**: An open model usually means its weights are publicly downloadable, while a fully open model goes further by disclosing the source code, training data organization, data preprocessing, and how data is fed into the model. That level of transparency is rare because training data is often considered proprietary, and Nvidia's Nemotron is cited as one of the few other prominent fully open stacks. Releasing multiple model sizes as a family lets users choose the best trade-off between capability and compute cost, which is the pattern K2 Horizon follows.

## [Microsoft to Enable Memory Integrity Protection by Default on Windows 11](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/windows-itpro-blog/expanding-memory-integrity-protection-across-windows-devices/4551984) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Microsoft announced it will enable memory integrity protection (also known as HVCI) by default on eligible Windows 11 devices starting with the October 13, 2026 Patch Tuesday. This move is intended to reduce the risk of malicious drivers hijacking the system. Default-enabling memory integrity raises the baseline security of Windows 11 by helping block kernel-level driver exploits. IT administrators and enterprises will need to assess driver compatibility and prepare for the change. Eligibility requires hardware virtualization support, UEFI and Secure Boot. Outdated or incompatible drivers may stop the feature from being enabled, and in rare cases could lead to blue screen errors. The rollout is scheduled to begin on the October 2026 Patch Tuesday.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 3, 06:09

**Background**: Memory integrity, or Hypervisor-protected Code Integrity (HVCI), uses hardware virtualization to create an isolated environment where only trusted kernel-mode code and drivers can run. It is part of Windows 11's virtualization-based security (VBS) features and helps defend against rootkits and other high-privilege malicious drivers. Default-enabling such protections reflects the broader industry trend of strengthening system security even when compatibility or performance trade-offs may occur.

## [US to Waive AI Chip Tariffs for Big Tech to Secure TSMC Investment](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43580) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The Trump administration plans to exempt Amazon, Google, and Microsoft from import tariffs on AI chips, tying the exemption to TSMC's level of U.S. investment. The proposal is still being refined and has not yet been signed by the president. This policy could lower AI infrastructure costs for major U.S. tech firms while encouraging TSMC to expand domestic chip production. It reflects how trade policy is being used to reshape the semiconductor supply chain and boost U.S. manufacturing. Under the U.S.-Taiwan trade agreement, Taiwanese companies that invest in the U.S. can receive tariff relief proportional to their production capacity, and TSMC may allocate that quota to American customers. The exemption plan remains under negotiation and is subject to change.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 3, 07:00

**Background**: The U.S. had threatened steep tariffs on advanced chips made in Taiwan, but in January 2026 Washington and Taipei reached a trade deal that reduced reciprocal tariffs on most Taiwanese goods from 20% to 15%. The deal also introduced quota mechanisms to lower tariff costs and risks for semiconductors imported into the U.S. The new proposal would extend these benefits to U.S. tech giants importing AI chips, contingent on TSMC expanding its U.S. operations.
