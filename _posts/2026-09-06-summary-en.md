## [SGLang v0.5.19 Adds Qwen3.8, Ling-3.0, Beam Search and DeepEP v2](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/releases/tag/v0.5.19) ⭐️ 7.0/10

SGLang released version v0.5.19, incorporating 786 pull requests from 214 contributors. The release adds support for models including Qwen3.8, Qwen3.8-27B, Ling-3.0-flash, Ling-3.0-tiny, Spark2.5, Granite 4.2, and MiniCPM-SALA, and introduces beam search, DeepEP v2, and LayerNorm sequence parallelism. SGLang is a widely used open-source LLM serving engine, and this release meaningfully broadens its support for popular new MoE models while improving serving efficiency on modern hardware. These advances make it easier for developers and enterprises to deploy high-performance, self-hosted inference for demanding reasoning and agentic workloads. Beam search is now available by passing a beam_width parameter, but it does not yet work with speculative decoding, disaggregation, DP attention, or HiCache. DeepEP v2's ElasticBuffer backend is exposed as --moe-a2a-backend deepep_v2 for FP8 MoE models, and LayerNorm sequence parallelism is currently limited to dense Qwen3 models, reducing Qwen3-8B prefill time by 3.5% on H100 and 5.6% on B200.

**Background**: SGLang is an open-source inference framework designed for low-latency, high-throughput serving of large language and multimodal models, scaling from a single GPU to large distributed clusters. ## [Paper Argues LLMs Act as Cognitive Viruses Spreading Memes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03344) ⭐️ 7.0/10

An arXiv paper titled 'LLMs as a Cognitive Virus' proposes that large language models behave like cognitive viruses, propagating memetic thought patterns through the ideas they generate and amplify. It has drawn broad Hacker News discussion, with the thread reaching 181 points and 152 comments. This framing shifts attention from what LLMs can do to how they shape human cognition and culture, raising questions about influence, manipulation, and responsibility. It may be useful for policymakers and researchers grappling with the societal effects of AI-generated content at scale. The paper applies memetics—the evolutionary study of ideas as replicating units—to generative AI, suggesting LLM outputs can act as high-volume vehicles for meme spread. Critics in the discussion note that the viral framing is not new, since any widespread idea or technology can be described as a virus.

**Background**: The term 'meme' was coined by Richard Dawkins to describe cultural units that spread by imitation, and memetics is the field that studies how ideas replicate and evolve. LLMs are particularly relevant to this view because they generate language at scale and are increasingly inserted into human conversations and workflows, potentially serving as efficient transmitters of memetic content.

## [NVIDIA Releases PAIR Software to Turn Idle Home PCs into Local AI Clusters](https://www.techspot.com/news/113742-nvidia-pair-software-turns-idle-home-computers-local.html) ⭐️ 7.0/10

NVIDIA has released PAIR (Personal AI Router), an open-source beta software that connects GeForce RTX PCs, DGX Spark systems, and Macs into a local AI cluster for distributed inference. The software requires no dedicated cables and can be set up within minutes. PAIR lets consumers pool idle home computing power, which NVIDIA estimates at roughly 165 teraFLOPS, for privacy-preserving AI inference without sending data to the cloud. This could reduce reliance on cloud AI services and make edge AI clusters practical for households and small teams. PAIR discovers participating devices on the same local network, manages supported inference engines, and presents an Ollama-compatible and OpenAI-compatible API endpoint. It supports Windows systems with RTX GPUs, macOS and Linux devices, as well as DGX Spark, and works with backends such as Ollama and LM Studio.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 5, 02:55

**Background**: Local AI inference runs models on users' own hardware, keeping data private but limiting performance to a single device's resources. Distributed inference spreads a model's workload across multiple machines, which traditionally requires specialized high-bandwidth interconnect. PAIR acts as a 'personal AI router' that routes inference requests to the most suitable idle device on the home network, abstracting away cluster complexity.

## [US Connected-Vehicle Rule Takes Effect; Automakers Drop Chinese Suppliers](https://t.me/zaihuapd/43623) ⭐️ 7.0/10

The U.S. Commerce Department's BIS connected-vehicle rule took effect on March 17, 2025, prohibiting imports and sales of connected vehicles and key components that rely on covered software or components from designated foreign adversaries such as China and Russia. Global automakers including Tesla and suppliers such as Pirelli are rushing to restructure supply chains and relocate software development teams to comply. This regulation forces a sweeping restructuring of the global automotive supply chain, because connected-vehicle systems and automated-driving software have become deeply integrated with Chinese-made components. It also sets a precedent for using U.S. export controls to counter perceived intelligence risks from vehicle cameras, GPS and data links. Under the phased timeline, sales of model-year 2027 vehicles with covered vehicle connectivity or automated-driving systems supplied by China or Russia will be prohibited, with further restrictions planned. Meanwhile, Pirelli is reportedly considering divesting shares or isolating its U.S. operations, and replacement suppliers such as Eagle Wireless offer components that cost noticeably more than Chinese equivalents.

telegram · zaihuapd · Sep 5, 10:04

**Background**: Connected vehicles are cars equipped with internet access, telematics, cameras, GPS and automated-driving systems that collect and transmit road and driver data. The Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security issued the final rule in January 2025 under the ICTS supply-chain authority, designating China and Russia as foreign adversaries and citing espionage risks from these systems. The rule restricts both the import and sale of affected vehicles, applying to hardware, software and services supplied by covered entities.
