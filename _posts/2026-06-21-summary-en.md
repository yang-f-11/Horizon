## [3D Fiber Micro-Tweezer Achieves 100,000x Force of Optical Tweezers](https://www.stdaily.com/web/gdxw/2026-06/19/content_534836.html) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers from Anhui University and the University of Science and Technology of China have developed a 3D fiber micro-tweezer using femtosecond laser composite manufacturing. The device, published in Nature on June 17, can precisely manipulate microscale objects with a force over 100,000 times greater than conventional optical tweezers. This breakthrough enables high-precision, low-damage, and programmable 3D manipulation of microscale objects, including single cells, within narrow spaces like 100 micrometers. It provides a new technological pathway for life sciences research and minimally invasive medicine. The micro-tweezer integrates light transmission, photothermal conversion, material response, and microstructural mechanical output into a single optical fiber. By adjusting the input light power, the force can be continuously and precisely controlled, reaching over 100,000 times the force of traditional optical tweezers.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 15:19

**Background**: Optical tweezers use a highly focused laser beam to trap and manipulate microscopic particles, but they typically exert very weak forces (piconewtons) and cannot handle opaque objects. Femtosecond laser composite manufacturing utilizes ultra-short laser pulses to precisely ablate and modify materials, enabling the creation of complex microstructures on fiber tips. This new fiber micro-tweezer overcomes the limitations of both traditional optical tweezers and mechanical micro-grippers.

## [HTTP QUERY Method Proposed for Safe Queries with Body](https://httpwg.org/http-extensions/draft-ietf-httpbis-safe-method-w-body.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The IETF HTTP Working Group has proposed a new HTTP QUERY method, described in draft-ietf-httpbis-safe-method-w-body, that allows clients to send safe, idempotent queries with a request body, addressing the limitations of URI length in GET requests and enabling caching. This proposal could significantly improve web API design by providing a standardized way to perform complex queries without exceeding URI length limits or compromising cacheability, benefiting developers building RESTful services and GraphQL APIs. The draft defines a new 'Accept-Query' response header for servers to advertise supported query formats, and the method is designed to be both safe and idempotent, allowing automatic retries. The draft expires in December 2026 and is still under active discussion.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 20, 06:28

**Background**: Traditional HTTP methods have limitations: GET is safe and idempotent but has URI length constraints, while POST can have a body but is not safe or idempotent, complicating caching and retries. The QUERY method fills this gap by combining the benefits of both. It is part of the ongoing evolution of the HTTP protocol to better support modern web applications.

## [Ultrasonic Espresso Brews at Room Temperature, Cuts Energy by 75%](https://www.wired.com/story/scientists-brew-espresso-with-ultrasonic-waves/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Scientists at the University of New South Wales developed an ultrasonic espresso brewing method that uses high-frequency sound waves to extract coffee at room temperature, achieving about 75% energy savings compared to traditional espresso machines. This breakthrough could revolutionize coffee brewing by significantly reducing energy consumption while maintaining taste quality, potentially enabling new multifunctional coffee machines. The ultrasonic system uses 100 watts of power and requires about three minutes of extraction time with fine grind, producing comparable concentration and extraction yield to traditional espresso. In a sensory test with 100 participants, the ultrasonic espresso showed negligible differences in aroma, flavor, and overall acceptability.

telegram · zaihuapd · Jun 21, 01:34

**Background**: Traditional espresso brewing requires hot water (typically 90-96°C) and high pressure to extract soluble compounds from coffee grounds quickly. Ultrasonic cavitation creates microscopic bubbles that collapse and generate microjets, physically breaking coffee particles and accelerating extraction even at room temperature, which normally would require 12-24 hours for cold brew.
