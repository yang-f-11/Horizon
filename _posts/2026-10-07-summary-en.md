## [OpenAI publishes AI-generated math proofs claiming open-problem solutions](https://openai.com/index/sharing-ai-progress-in-mathematics/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

OpenAI published a GitHub repository (openai/math) containing mathematical manuscripts and supporting proof artifacts, including Lean formalizations, produced by an internal frontier model. The collection claims full or partial solutions to a set of long-standing open problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, following an earlier OpenAI post on "ten advances" in the field. This is one of the most prominent public claims by a major AI lab that model-generated work has contributed to genuinely open research problems, potentially reshaping how mathematicians use AI as a research tool. The announcement drew intense scrutiny — over 500 points and 450 comments — because verifying such claims touches on peer review, proof correctness, and the sociology of mathematical research. The repository states the model was evaluated on open research problems after performance on existing mathematical evaluations saturated, and that some outputs build on earlier results produced by the models. Commenters noting the collection claims complete solutions to roughly 90 of the top 500 open problems, with high-profile entries such as Hilbert's tenth problem over ℚ, Unique Games, Baum–Connes, Hadwiger, Landau–Siegel zeros, and Barnette's Conjecture — though the depth of verification and the significance of each result vary.

hackernews · OfficialTurkey · Oct 6, 22:17 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49984923)

**Background**: Automated theorem proving is a long-standing subfield of automated reasoning in which computer programs generate or check mathematical proofs, and it was a major motivation for the development of computer science itself. Modern efforts often rely on interactive proof assistants such as Lean, which let a proof be written in a formal language that a machine can verify step by step, so a "formalization" is much stronger evidence than a natural-language argument. OpenAI's post is framed around open problems — questions mathematicians have not yet resolved — rather than benchmark puzzles with known answers.

## [Francis Halzen Wins 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for IceCube Detector](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2026/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Francis Halzen, the principal investigator of the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, was awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for conceiving the cubic-kilometer-scale neutrino detector buried in the Antarctic ice and for the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. The Nobel citation credits his "decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin." The award recognizes the birth of neutrino astronomy: IceCube showed that neutrinos, not just photons, can be used as messengers to observe the most violent processes in the universe, which photons cannot escape. It also validates decades of large-scale, high-risk infrastructure science in one of the harshest environments on Earth, and is likely to strengthen funding arguments for successor projects such as IceCube-Gen2. IceCube was completed on 18 December 2010 and consists of thousands of digital optical modules (DOMs) deployed on vertical strings at depths of about 1,450 to 2,450 meters in holes melted with a hot-water drill; it detects neutrinos indirectly, via the Cherenkov radiation emitted when charged particles produced in neutrino interactions travel faster than the speed of light in the ice (which is possible because that speed is below the vacuum speed of light). An upgrade approved in 2019 was announced on 12 February 2026 to have been successfully deployed, marking the observatory's first major expansion in 15 years.

hackernews · solarist · Oct 6, 09:48 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49976265)

**Background**: A neutrino is an elementary subatomic particle with no electric charge and an extremely small mass that interacts only through the weak nuclear force and gravity, which is why it is nicknamed the "ghost particle" — trillions can pass through an entire planet without interacting. Neutrinos are produced in enormous numbers by nuclear reactions inside stars, by supernovae, and by radioactive decay, and about 65 billion solar neutrinos pass through every square centimeter of the Earth's surface each second. Because they barely interact, they can escape from the dense cores of stars and exploding stars and travel in straight lines across the cosmos, carrying information that light cannot. IceCube exploits a cubic kilometer of clear Antarctic ice as its detection medium, using the ice itself as both target and sensor volume.

## [Mistral Large 4 preview: a 1T-parameter MoE model with promised open weights](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/6/le-chonk/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Mistral AI released a preview of Mistral Large 4, codenamed "Le chonk", a 1 trillion parameter mixture-of-experts model with 49 billion active parameters, trained on Mistral's own cluster of 3,800 NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GPUs. The preview is available through the Mistral API, with open weights promised for release at the "end of this month". This marks a strong comeback for Mistral after Mistral Large 3, which scored only 9 on Artificial Analysis; Mistral Large 4 now scores 38, putting a European lab roughly six months behind the frontier rather than far off the pace. An open-weights release of a trillion-parameter model would also make frontier-scale capacity far more accessible to the open ecosystem. The API exposes only two reasoning levels, "none" and "high", and testing suggested the setting made little practical difference — the "high" mode produced fewer output tokens (2,717) than "none" (3,275). Artificial Analysis still places it behind DeepSeek 4.1 Flash, a 552B model, so it is competitive but not frontier-leading.

rss · Simon Willison · Oct 6, 20:18

**Background**: Mixture-of-experts (MoE) is a machine learning technique that splits a model into many specialized sub-networks called "experts"; only a few experts activate for each token, so a model can have a huge total parameter count while computing with far fewer "active" parameters. This is why Mistral Large 4 can have 1 trillion total parameters yet only 49 billion active ones, keeping inference cost and speed closer to a much smaller dense model. Mistral AI is a French AI lab known for releasing open-weight models, and NVIDIA's Grace Blackwell is its current-generation GPU platform used for large-scale AI training.

## [OpenAI opens Decisions API to public beta](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/decisions) ⭐️ 8.0/10

OpenAI has launched its Decisions API in public beta, exposing a dedicated endpoint for fast yes/no, option-selection, and confidence-scored outputs built on the GPT-6 Luna model. The API is designed for narrow decision tasks such as checking conditions, picking from a fixed set of options, and scoring text or images against a rubric. A first-party API for cheap, high-volume decision calls signals that OpenAI sees lightweight 'System One' style inference as a distinct product category, putting direct pressure on specialized inference providers and open-source classifiers. It also fuels the debate over whether frontier AI is becoming a commodity market, where price competition rather than raw capability drives adoption. The API targets fixed-choice and rubric-scoring tasks rather than open-ended generation, and notably it does not appear to support prompt caching at launch, which some developers say would have been valuable for long system prompts and bulk data processing. Pricing is positioned well below general-purpose chat models, reflecting the far smaller output token footprint of a single decision.

hackernews · chiefstorm · Oct 6, 20:57 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49984025)

**Background**: In modern LLM applications, a huge share of calls are not creative writing but simple branching: does this text match a category, which of these five labels applies, how confidently does this image fit a rubric. Running those through a general chat model is wasteful because the prompt is long but the answer is one token, so vendors have started offering models purpose-built for constrained decisions. An API like this lets developers replace hand-rolled prompt engineering and fine-tuned classifiers with a single standardized endpoint.

## [EmbeddingGemma 2: Google's Open Lightweight Multimodal Embedding Model](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/embeddinggemma-2/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Google released EmbeddingGemma 2, an open embedding model under the Apache 2.0 license that is lightweight — around 270M parameters for text and roughly 440M for text plus vision — and natively maps text, code, images, video, and audio into a unified 768-dimensional embedding space. It follows the earlier EmbeddingGemma release and is positioned as the most capable model for on-device multimodal embeddings. Embedding models are the backbone of search, retrieval and routing systems, and until now developers had few good options in the moderate-size range — especially open ones that can run locally. An Apache 2.0-licensed multimodal model from Google gives teams a durable, self-hostable alternative to proprietary hosted embedding APIs, where a vendor can retire a model and silently invalidate millions of stored vectors. The model outputs into a single 768-dimensional space, so the same index can serve text-to-image search, voice-memo-to-video-moment retrieval, and other cross-modal tasks on everyday devices; a notable but under-promoted feature is a multimodal input decision-making API, listed only as a third example in the announcement. Community members note that faster local embedding tooling is already being calibrated for it, suggesting an emerging local-first workflow.

hackernews · ilreb · Oct 6, 16:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49980487)

**Background**: Embedding models convert content such as text or images into numeric vectors, so that semantically similar items end up close together in vector space; this is what powers semantic search, recommendations, clustering, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines. Gemma is Google's family of lightweight open models derived from the same technology behind its Gemini models, first released in February 2024 in 2B and 7B parameter sizes. EmbeddingGemma applies that lineage to the embedding task, and the "2" version extends it from text-only to multimodal input.

## [OpenTPU: An open-source AI accelerator designed by AI itself](https://github.com/FeSens/openTPU) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A GitHub project called OpenTPU (FeSens/openTPU) has released an open-source AI inference accelerator that its author says was developed using AI-driven recursive self-improvement, the same technique previously applied to generating RISC-V CPU cores. According to the developer, the design started out producing only a few tokens per second and, through repeated self-improvement iterations, reached 80+ tokens/sec on smaller models. If accurate, this is a concrete example of AI systems contributing to the design of the hardware that runs them, a loop that could compress accelerator design cycles that normally take large human teams many months. It also matters for the open-source hardware ecosystem, since it suggests smaller players could get custom inference silicon without a commercial chip design pipeline. The project is described as an open-source inference engine able to run many modern models such as Qwen 3.5 and Gemma 4, and it is tagged as a TPU-style FPGA-oriented accelerator rather than a general GPU replacement; the token-rate figures cited refer to smaller models, and the performance claims have not yet been independently validated. Community speculation also raised the more ambitious question of whether an AI given a large FPGA could design model architectures that exploit the reconfigurable fabric.

hackernews · fsbonetto · Oct 6, 16:23 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49980715)

**Background**: Recursive self-improvement (RSI) describes a process in which an AI system rewrites and tests its own code, improving its own capabilities; it has long been discussed as a path toward rapid intelligence growth and as a major safety concern, though research through 2026 shows no sign of an actual intelligence explosion. TPU stands for Tensor Processing Unit, a chip specialized for neural-network math, in contrast to general-purpose GPUs. FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array) chips can be reconfigured after manufacturing, which makes them a popular low-volume, low-latency alternative for edge AI inference where GPUs may be overkill.

## [Honda and Obayashi Develop In-Motion Wireless EV Charging Tech](https://china.kyodonews.net/articles/-/16535) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Honda and Obayashi Corporation announced they have jointly developed foundational technology for wirelessly supplying power to pure electric vehicles while they are in motion, with a vehicle reportedly able to receive up to 150 kW instantaneously when passing over road-embedded power units at about 80 km/h. The two companies plan to conduct demonstration tests on the Tateyama Expressway in Chiba Prefecture from fiscal 2027 onward. If it works at scale, dynamic charging could shrink the battery packs and charging downtime needed by logistics and transport fleets, addressing one of the biggest barriers to electrifying heavy commercial vehicles. It also puts Honda and a Japanese construction giant into a global race for road-embedded charging, alongside projects such as Purdue University and the Indiana Department of Transportation's highway segment that wirelessly charged a heavy-duty truck in late 2025. The 150 kW figure is an instantaneous peak delivered during the brief pass over a ground power unit at roughly 80 km/h, not a sustained charging rate, and the announcement covers foundational technology rather than a finished system. Practical questions such as power-transfer efficiency, tolerance for coil misalignment, the cost of digging transmitters into highways, and cross-industry standardization remain unresolved, and Honda says it is aiming for real-world deployment in logistics and transport while Obayashi stresses the importance of developing alongside automakers and integrating the technology into related systems.

telegram · zaihuapd · Oct 6, 08:18

**Background**: Dynamic wireless charging (also called dynamic wireless power transfer) works by embedding transmitter coils in the roadway that send energy through a magnetic field to receiver coils mounted on the underside of a vehicle, letting it charge without stopping. This differs from static wireless charging, which only works while a car is parked over a pad, and from conventional plug-in fast charging. Research and pilot projects worldwide have tested such road-embedded coils, including an Indiana highway segment unveiled in December 2025 that wirelessly charged a heavy-duty electric truck as it drove.

## [sub2api Reports Payment Flaw: Forged EasyPay Callbacks Enable Free Recharge](https://github.com/Wei-Shaw/sub2api/issues/7881) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A GitHub issue (#7881) filed against the sub2api project reports that an attacker can reuse the signature generated when placing an order to forge an EasyPay payment-success callback, causing an unpaid recharge order to be marked as paid without knowing the merchant key. The issue, still open at the time of reporting, includes a described attack chain and concrete fix suggestions, and its author recommends switching away from popup mode as a temporary mitigation. If the report holds up, any registered user with permission to create recharge orders on a deployment that uses EasyPay in popup mode could obtain credits for free, turning a signature-handling bug into direct financial loss for operators. It also illustrates a recurring class of business-logic flaw in payment integrations, where callback notification endpoints are trusted without proper signature verification or amount checks. The reported root causes are signature-string concatenation without escaping, unsanitized query parameters carried in return_url, exposure of the signature in popup mode, and lax validation of callback parameters; the described exploit relies on the fact that trade_status occupies a byte-identical position in the reordered callback signature string as in the order-creation signature. The PoC is described as a purely local unit test that does not touch the network, and since the issue remains open the vulnerability has not yet been independently verified or patched.

**Background**: EasyPay is a type of third-party aggregator payment platform that lets site owners and developers accept Alipay and WeChat Pay without signing a formal merchant contract, notifying the merchant's server asynchronously when a payment succeeds. Merchants normally rely on a merchant key and a signature over the callback parameters to confirm that a 'payment succeeded' notification genuinely came from the gateway, rather than from an attacker who simply POSTs to the callback URL. A 'popup mode' integration opens the payment page in a browser popup, which can expose the signing parameters to the client side and make them easier to reuse. The sub2api project is hosted on GitHub, and the disclosed issue concerns the payment/recharge flow of its deployment.
