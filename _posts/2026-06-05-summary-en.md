## [Chinese token cost varies: Anthropic 71% more than OpenAI, Chinese models efficient](https://x.com/arankomatsuzaki/status/2049125048792006965) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A research comparison reveals that Anthropic's tokenizer uses 71% more tokens for Chinese text than OpenAI's, and up to 3.24x more for Hindi and 2.86x for Arabic. Chinese models like Qwen and Kimi handle Chinese more efficiently than English. This finding has direct cost implications for multilingual AI applications, as token usage directly affects API pricing. Users and developers may need to choose models based on language-specific token efficiency to optimize costs. The analysis used translations of 'The Bitter Lesson' and tested multiple model-language pairs. Gemini and Qwen showed the lowest non-English overhead, while Anthropic had the highest and Kimi the second highest. Hindi, despite its large speaker population, had the worst token efficiency among tested languages.

**Background**: Large language models (LLMs) process text through tokenizers that convert text into tokens, which are the units the model predicts and prices are based on. Different tokenizers use different algorithms (e.g., Byte-Pair Encoding, SentencePiece) and vocabulary sizes, leading to varying token counts for the same text in different languages. Non-Latin script languages like Chinese, Hindi, and Arabic often require more tokens because their characters are not as efficiently represented as Latin characters in the tokenizer's vocabulary.
